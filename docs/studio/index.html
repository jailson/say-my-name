say-my-name / studio

Record or upload your name

Record yourself, or bring an audio file you made elsewhere. Trim it, download it, paste the snippet. Everything happens in this tab — the audio never leaves your device, there is no account, and there is no server to upload to.

1 · Your name

Used to build the snippet. Upload the files there yourself.

2 · Pronunciations

Add more than one if your name has a native pronunciation and a local one. Both are correct, and the widget will offer both.

Rather not publish your own voice? Use a synthesized one instead — it's a legitimate choice, and the widget labels it honestly.

3 · Preview

This is the real component, running on what you just recorded.

4 · Copy the snippet

The snippet assumes the component is already on the page:
<script type="module" src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/say-my-name@0"></script>

Don't want to record your own voice?

That's a reasonable position — a clean, isolated sample of your voice on a public page is exactly what voice-cloning models want, and you don't owe anyone one. A synthesized voice you picked deliberately and labelled honestly is a perfectly good answer. Generate a clip elsewhere, then bring it back here with Use an audio file and tick This voice is synthesized.

Only the first one lets your IPA actually drive the sound; the rest ignore it, so you tune by ear. Robotic-but-exact often beats smooth-but-wrong for a name.

Where the file goes on your site

Download it here, then drop it anywhere your site serves static files — public/ or static/ in most frameworks, or any folder in a plain HTML site. Point audio at that path. Hosting it on another domain (S3, a CDN) works too: audio elements don't need CORS headers to play.
