say-my-name / studio

Record or upload your name

Record yourself, or bring an audio file you made elsewhere. Trim it, download it, paste the snippet. Everything happens in this tab — the audio never leaves your device, there is no account, and there is no server to upload to.

2 · Pronunciations

Add more than one if your name has a native pronunciation and a local one. Both are correct, and the widget will offer both.

The IPA and respelling fill in by themselves from the name and language as you type. The first one downloads eSpeak (~9 MB) and says so while it runs; after that it is instant. Nothing you type is ever sent anywhere — eSpeak runs in this tab. Suggest from spelling additionally generates the audio.

Filling automatically from the name and language. Press Suggest from spelling to also generate the audio. Type your own IPA or respelling and that field becomes yours — it stops being overwritten.

Rather not publish your own voice? Use a synthesized one instead — it's a legitimate choice, and the widget labels it honestly.

3 · Where the audio lives

Download the files from step 2, then drop them anywhere your site serves static content — public/ or static/ in most frameworks, or any folder in a plain HTML site. This path is what goes into the snippet. Hosting on another domain (S3, a CDN) works too: audio elements don't need CORS headers to play.

Don't want to record your own voice?

That's a reasonable position — a clean, isolated sample of your voice on a public page is exactly what voice-cloning models want, and you don't owe anyone one. A synthesized voice you picked deliberately and labelled honestly is a perfectly good answer.

The quickest route is Suggest from spelling above — it runs eSpeak in this tab, fills in the IPA and generates the audio, and ticks This voice is synthesized for you. The voice is robotic, but it is derived from real letter-to-sound rules rather than guessed at. If you want a better-sounding one, generate a clip with any of these and bring it back with Use an audio file:

Only the eSpeak-based ones connect your IPA to the sound; the natural-voice tools ignore it, so you tune those by ear. Robotic-but-exact often beats smooth-but-wrong for a name.
